Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how My SMS Alert (the "App") handles your information. Your privacy is a priority, and the App is designed to minimize data collection and maximize user control.

1. Information the App Processes

To provide its core functionality (sending emergency alerts), the App requests access to the following:

  • Location Data: When you trigger an alert, the App accesses your device's geographical coordinates (GPS) and may convert them into a physical address. This information is included in the SMS sent to your chosen recipients.
  • Phone Numbers: You provide the phone numbers of your trusted contacts. These are stored locally on your device.
  • SMS Services: On Android, the App sends messages directly via your carrier. On both iOS and Android, the App may use the Twilio API if configured by you.

2. Data Storage and Security

  • Local Storage: All configuration data, including recipient phone numbers and message templates, is stored locally on your device using encrypted storage where applicable.
  • No Cloud Harvesting: The Developer does not operate a central server to collect, store, or monitor your location history, contacts, or messages.
  • Third-Party Services: If you use the Twilio integration, your account credentials and transmission data are handled by Twilio according to their privacy policy.

3. Data Sharing

The App does not share your personal data with any third parties, except for:

  • The recipients you explicitly choose to notify when you press the alert button.
  • The telecommunications provider or SMS gateway (like Twilio) required to transmit the message.

4. Permissions

The App requires specific system permissions to function:

  • Location: To determine where you are during an emergency.
  • SMS (Android): To send automated messages via your SIM card.
  • Internet: To perform address lookups and communicate with the Twilio API.

5. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. All data remains on the user's device.

6. Changes to This Policy

The Developer may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by a new "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the official App Store/Google Play support channels.
